Hearth Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-28

Hearth is a private household assistant run by its owner for their own family. Its only Google user is the owner.

What data is accessed

With the owner's consent, Hearth uses read-only access to the owner's Gmail (gmail.readonly) and Google Calendar (calendar.readonly). It cannot send, delete or change mail or calendar events.

How it is used

Mail and calendar data are used only to help the owner keep track of household matters. They are processed on the owner's own computers and are not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or model training.

Storage and removal

Data stays on the owner's own servers. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Hearth's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
